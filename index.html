WITHIN

How to play

Every answer hides a smaller word inside it. The clue hints at both words.

Example

Sit down dinner

SEAT

"Sit down" points to SEAT. "Dinner" points to EAT, hiding inside it. The lavender tiles show where the hidden word sits.

EasyMediumHardone new round of each, every day
  • A new round of three puzzles arrives every day at midnight. Missed a day? Past rounds wait in the Archive.
  • You get 3 guesses per puzzle.
  • Guess the full word, or tap the lavender tiles to guess the hidden word. A correct hidden word fills in its tiles.
  • Your last guess must be the full word.
  • Each puzzle is worth up to 6 points. Every guess that isn't the final answer costs 1 point, and so does each clue.
  • One clue unlocks per guess: before guess 1, before guess 2 and before guess 3. Unused clues stay available.
  • Clue 1 shows the first hidden-word letter, clue 2 the first letter, clue 3 the last letter. A clue whose letter is already showing can't be used and costs nothing.
  • Guesses must have the right number of letters to count.
